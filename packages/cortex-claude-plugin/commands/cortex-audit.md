The standalone core has no automated compliance or enforcement audit endpoint. Use `cortex_status` for counts, `cortex_search` for relevant records, `cortex_get` for explicit revisions, and `cortex_graph` for typed links within the exact repository. Cite ID, revision, source and authority. Distinguish inspected evidence from unverified coverage or model use. Do not call legacy cortex_audit tools or claim a complete corpus audit from a bounded query.
